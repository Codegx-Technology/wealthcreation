Featured Speakers

Learn from industry leaders and spiritual mentors

Dr. Cindy Trimm

CEO & Transformational Leader

Dr. Cindy Trimm is a former senator and CEO of a conglomerate of companies. Trained in Strategic Leadership at Oxford University's Saïd Business School and Corporate Governance from Harvard University. She is listed among the world's top 500 Leaders of Influence and has received multiple honors including the Life Time Achievement Award from President Barack Obama. Founder of The Trimm Institute for Global Leadership, she has authored over 30 books including bestsellers Commanding Your Morning and The Rules of Engagement for Spiritual Warfare.

Jonathan Jackson

Chairman - Lordship Group

Chairman of Lordship Group, a leading real estate development company with over 7 million ft² of property valued at $1.5 billion USD across Central Europe and East Africa. With 30+ years of experience, Lordship delivers award-winning mixed-use developments including hotels, retail, residential and commercial properties. Today, Lordship‘s portfolio reflects the aspirations of a much broader range of investment opportunities, encompassing medium to large-scale mixed-used schemes of hotel & leisure, retail, residential and commercial developments.

Caroline Marsh

Christian Entrepreneur & Speaker

Caroline Marsh is an accomplished female property investor in the United Kingdom. Recognized for her achievements, she has been featured on BBC prime time TV Channel 4 as 'The Secret Millionaire.' Caroline continues to make a significant impact in the media as a Personal Financial Educator, Property Investor, Entrepreneur, and Inspirational Speaker

Joanne Muturi

Life & Business Coach

Life and business coach dedicated to empowering women to achieve clarity in their life direction and reach their full potential. She focuses on helping women in corporate settings align their lives with their unique purpose.

Mr. Eduardo Ramos

Co-Founder, Nuova Corporation

Mr. Ramos is part of a globally experienced leadership team with strong backgrounds in energy and mining. Nuova specializes in responsible mineral extraction and strategic investments in critical supply chains. The company supports the global energy transition by delivering essential commodities and promoting sustainable development across energy and mining sectors..

Dr Shan Rajegopal

Board Director, El Camino Duro Investments

Dr Shan Rajegopal, a Singapore-born British citizen, has 40 years of experience in project, program, and portfolio management, overseeing multi-million dollar portfolios globally. He is a board director for El Camino Duro Investments Limited, funding private and public projects worldwide. In this capacity, he has set up SPVs, project governance, funding, and oversight for complex projects including hotels in Neom (Saudi Arabia), mixed developments at Jeddah Airport, resorts in UAE, and renewable energy projects in Saudi Arabia, Ghana, Nigeria, and India. He is also a strategic advisor for Al-Ruqi Company (gold and jewellery, Saudi Arabia), working closely with the Ministry of Minerals and the Central Bank. Shan collaborates with HNWIs, investment groups, funding consortiums, lawyers, consultants, and contractors.

Arinze Nwanna - MC

Investment Professional & Master of Ceremony

Dynamic leader and investment professional with background in global finance and real estate private equity. Former J.P. Morgan investment banker, now leading strategic acquisitions at a UK-based private equity firm. Graduate of UCL and Imperial, serving in pastoral leadership since age 20.
